### WhyStrohm

We make films, posts and campaigns for founder-led companies. These are the two tools
we built for that work and give away.

---

## Shotkit

**Ship on-brand video at scale.** Plan every shot on a board, check every frame, render every
size. New in 3.7: Shotkit Canvas, where every clip plays and shots move.

[![Shotkit demo: a brief typed in Claude Code, every shot planned and every frame checked, the rough cut, then a board where the clips play and a shot is moved, and one cut in every size](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/whystrohm/shotkit/main/docs/images/demo.webp)](https://github.com/whystrohm/shotkit)

Give it your website and one sentence. It plans every shot in your brand, shows the price
before anything is made, checks each frame and cuts the video in every size. On your Mac,
with your own keys.

In Claude Code:

```
/plugin install shotkit --marketplace whystrohm/shotkit
```

[Repo](https://github.com/whystrohm/shotkit) · [Watch a real run](https://whystrohm.com/blog/shotkit-canvas-one-real-run) · [Open its board](https://whystrohm.com/shotkit/examples/whystrohm-how-we-work/board.html)

## Digital Twin

**A digital twin that knows your work.** Your decisions, voice rules and limits, in one place
your coding sessions can ask. New in 4.0: the twin app, with a head built from your photo.

[![A digital twin that knows your work. Three inputs go in: your photo becomes the head, your repos are read, your notes fill it. Then it answers a question from the notes and stops at a limit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/whystrohm/digital-twin-of-yourself/main/assets/twin-cover.webp)](https://github.com/whystrohm/digital-twin-of-yourself)

Give it one photo, the repos you pick and your notes. It answers with the note it came from,
checks a draft against your voice rules and stops at the limits you set. On your machine.
The demo twin is a made-up person.

```
git clone https://github.com/whystrohm/digital-twin-of-yourself.git
cd digital-twin-of-yourself
python3 twin/twin.py
```

[Repo](https://github.com/whystrohm/digital-twin-of-yourself) · [How it works](https://github.com/whystrohm/digital-twin-of-yourself/blob/main/docs/TWIN.md)
